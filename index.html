Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does TMX-QUANTUM use the TON blockchain?

TMX-QUANTUM is built for The Open Network (TON) ecosystem inside Telegram. Your mined 💎 balance and task achievements link to your Telegram identity and TON Connect wallet for on-chain reward eligibility and ecosystem utility.

How does the 7-day login reward work?

Log in and claim daily for +200 💎 per day. If you claim consistently for 7 consecutive days, Day 7 awards your +200 💎 daily reward plus a +2,000 💎 7-day streak bonus. Missing a day resets your streak back to Day 1.

How do Spin & Win and Referrals work?

You get one free Spin & Win chance every day for up to +500 💎, and you earn +250 💎 per referral invite. Progress syncs with MongoDB Atlas with local fallback.

What if the 2X Ad Boost does not load?

Ad availability varies by region and network connection. If no ad fills, you can still submit your standard session without getting stuck.
