Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does TMX-QUANTUM connect with TON?

TMX-QUANTUM operates as a Telegram Mini App within the TON ecosystem. Your 💎 balance and quest progress associate with your Telegram profile and linked TON Connect wallet for future ecosystem features.

How does the 7-day check-in streak work?

Check in daily for +200 💎. Completing 7 consecutive days unlocks a +2,000 💎 bonus on Day 7 on top of the daily reward. Missing a day resets progress to Day 1.

How do Quantum Spin and referrals work?

You get one daily Quantum Spin for up to +500 💎 and earn +250 💎 per referred friend. Progress syncs with MongoDB Atlas with local storage fallback.

How do Telegram Stars purchases and subscriptions work?

Select a Pro subscription or credit pack in the Bonus tab to open Telegram's native Stars (XTR) checkout. You can manage or cancel 30-day auto-renewing subscriptions anytime in Telegram Settings → My Stars → Subscriptions, or send /paysupport to the bot.

What if the 2X Ad Boost does not fill?

Ad fill depends on network conditions and regional availability. If an ad is unavailable, you can still submit your standard round without interruption.
