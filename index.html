Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does a 15-second mining round work?

Tap the lightning button to start a 15-second round and tap repeatedly to build your Session count. When finished, tap Submit & Save Session to add points to your Balance.

How are user balances and leaderboard rankings stored?

Your user profile, balance, verified tasks, and leaderboard rank sync with your backend connected to MongoDB Atlas, while also caching locally so your progress stays safe if the network drops.

What if the 2X Ad Boost does not load?

Ad availability varies by region and network connection. If no ad fills, you can still submit your standard session without getting stuck.
